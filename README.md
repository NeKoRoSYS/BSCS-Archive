# Marky's BS CompSci Archive — 1st Year

Hello there! This GitHub repository serves as an archive of all my creations; in partial fulfillment of requirements for the courses I am enrolled to under the BS Computer Science program of Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Maynila.

<br>

## Why I am doing this

I have plenty of experience with software development years prior to stepping into college. I jumped straight into projects here and there, and never bothered with learning the whys and hows of Computer Science. I learned how to code and ship products, but I never got to learn the science behind them. There are areas and domains in the tech industry that I am yet to know about in order to develop the relevant skillsets; and that is why I think enrolling to BSCS would fill such gap. I am very eager to experience what it is like to learn the fundamentals, theories, data structures, and algorithms that any Computer Science graduate must know.

The school environment itself is a plus! I am notoriously bad at planning because I never got to—at the very least—finish whatever I have been working on. Enrolling to the BSCS college program will help me form the discipline that I need to become more or less a professional software engineer in the future.
