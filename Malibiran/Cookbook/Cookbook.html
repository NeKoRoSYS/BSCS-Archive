Adobong Manok

  • Adobong manok is a famous Filipino dish made of chicken pieces braised in a rich blend of vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, bay leaves, and whole black peppercorns. It offers a savory, tangy, and garlicky flavor profile and is traditionally served hot over a bed of white rice.

    • Cookies

  • A cookie is a small, flat, sweet baked treat made mainly from flour, sugar, and fat, such as butter or oil. Popular types include chocolate chip, sugar, and oatmeal cookies. They can be soft and chewy or crisp and crunchy.

    • Brownies

  • A chocolate brownie is a square-shaped, baked chocolate dessert bar with a dense texture and a shiny, crackly top crust.

    • Chocolate Cake

  • Chocolate cake is a rich, decadent dessert featuring a tender, moist crumb and a deep, sweet cocoa flavor. Often made with a base of flour, sugar, eggs, and cocoa powder—and commonly enhanced with buttermilk, oil, or hot coffee—it is usually paired with a thick, glossy chocolate buttercream or ganache.

    • Mango Graham

  • Mango graham, also known as mango float or crema de mangga, is a popular no-bake Filipino icebox cake made by layering graham crackers, sweetened cream, and ripe yellow mangoes.
